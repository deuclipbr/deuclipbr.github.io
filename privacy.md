# DeuClip - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

## 1. Who we are
DeuClip is a personal tool operated by an individual creator to cut videos and publish them to the creator's own social media accounts.

## 2. Data we access
When you connect a TikTok account through Login Kit, DeuClip receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) for that account, only after you authorize it.

## 3. How we use data
The access token is used only to upload or publish the videos that you choose to your own TikTok account through the Content Posting API. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## 4. Storage and sharing
The token is stored locally on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. You can disconnect at any time, and you can revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. To request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below.

## 6. Children
DeuClip is not directed to children under 13.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated at any time.
